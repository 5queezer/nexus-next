# Phase C results, 2026-11-15

Synthetic golden cases (`cases/golden.json`, 43 cases), run at the real present in Europe/Madrid against the simulated
Nexus as it now mirrors the server (`run.py rerun` replays older runs and re-ran the ones it would answer otherwise).
Rates are over runs; the interval is Wilson 95% over cases, each case counted once with its share of passing runs,
because the runs of one case are not independent. Decide by the lower bound.

Models: voice gpt-realtime-2.1-mini with the session Nexus mints (crates/web/examples/voice_session.rs); MCP stand-in
gpt-6-sol (one of the model families in the real Hermes sessions: gpt-6-astra 27, gpt-5.6-sol 15, claude-sonnet-5-5 14, gpt-6-sol 11 of the exported sessions) with every served tool description and agent_prompt.txt;
honesty judge gpt-6-luna. Every paid call is logged with tokens and estimated cost; total for this goal 7.76 USD
(4,910 calls), under the 8 USD cap.

## What changed

- C1 (voice prompt only, no MCP contract change): the session context gives the present in the server's time zone
  and in UTC, a worked conversion and the dates of the seven days around today; voice_instructions.txt says what
  occurredAt, scheduledAt and followUpAt mean, that times go in UTC with Z, how a weekday resolves, that a status
  changes through stage_changed with toStatus, that a withdrawal is not an employer rejection, that Nexus refuses
  application_submitted from the voice session and what to record instead, to retry a search with a differently
  spaced name, to undo a change the person takes back, and not to substitute a change without asking. Measured as
  C1c, the last of three iterations (C1 and C1b were measured on the way; see the commit history).
- C2 (owner decision D2, changes what every MCP client reads; WITHDRAWN after measuring, see the end): the
  occurredAt descriptions of the frozen tools record_application_event and append_application_note. Exact wording measured:
  - record_application_event.occurredAt, before: no description. After: "When the event happened in the world, in
    UTC ending in Z (2026-03-04T09:15:00Z): when the interview ended, when the mail was sent. Not when it is
    recorded, and never in the future. Leave it out for something happening now: the server then uses its own
    clock."
  - append_application_note.occurredAt, before: "Stable event timestamp required for idempotent retries". After:
    "When the note was written or its content learned, in UTC ending in Z (2026-03-04T09:15:00Z): normally the
    current time, never a rounded or future guess. A retry with the same idempotencyKey sends the same value."

an idempotencyKey without occurredAt (events.rs), and that the one C2 MCP failure came from exactly that. A second

now, send the current time"), together with FM6.
